Full project index

05 — Flagship cases · 01 / 06

The city, seen in motion

Computer vision and geolocation that turn urban routes into actionable information.

We process images captured from vehicles to identify public road conditions and place them on the map. The result combines visual evidence with georeferenced data to help prioritize surveys, maintenance and operational response.

Technology
Computer vision · Geolocation · Georeferenced data
Applications
Surveys · Maintenance · Operational response

05 — Flagship cases · 02 / 06

Visual intelligence for Retail Shelf

From a walk down the aisle to an intelligent reading of the retail shelf.

We combine image capture, visual recognition and text extraction to analyze products, facings and labels on the shelf. We turn point-of-sale images into structured information for auditing, retail execution and assortment analysis.

Technology
Image capture · Visual recognition · Text extraction
Applications
Auditing · Retail execution · Assortment analysis

05 — Flagship cases · 03 / 06

GeoAI for land, environment and agriculture

Satellites, GIS and artificial intelligence to detect patterns at a territorial scale.

We integrate satellite imagery, geographic data and vision models to identify areas of interest across large expanses. From detecting open-air dumps to analyzing land cover and productive zones, we turn geospatial information into maps that help guide research and decision-making.

Technology
Satellite imagery · GIS · Vision models
Applications
Open-air dumps · Land-cover analysis · Productive zones

05 — Flagship cases · 04 / 06

Aerial intelligence with drones

Aerial capture and computer vision to observe, map and analyze from new perspectives.

We combine aerial imagery with computer vision tools and geospatial analysis to build a detailed view of the territory. Drone workflows can be adapted to surveys, inspections and monitoring of hard-to-reach areas, integrating the captured data with maps and analytical models.

Technology
Aerial imagery · Computer vision · Geospatial analysis
Applications
Surveys · Inspections · Hard-to-reach areas

05 — Flagship cases · 05 / 06

Smart urban waste management

Computer vision to support the collection and municipal management of urban solid waste.

We analyze images from collection routes to detect scattered waste and other relevant conditions in public space. Visual information can help municipalities and operators monitor services, identify areas that need attention and improve the traceability of the operation.

Technology
Computer vision · Image analysis
Applications
Service monitoring · Areas that need attention · Traceability

05 — Flagship cases · 06 / 06

Smart inspection: quality and anomalies

Computer vision to verify products, detect deviations and support quality control.

We apply visual analysis to industrial checks such as counting, fill level, labeling and anomaly detection. The models help identify variations and raise review signals, integrating automated inspection with the quality criteria of each process.

Technology
Computer vision · Visual analysis
Applications
Counting · Fill level · Labeling · Anomaly detection
