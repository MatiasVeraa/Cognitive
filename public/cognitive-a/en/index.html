05 — Cases

From problem to system.

  1. Public safety · Crowded spaces

    Mass Facial Recognition

    Identify and follow people in public spaces, even after their face is no longer visible.

    Specification
    Up to 30 faces identified per second.
    Technology
    Computer Vision · Image Analysis · Face Recognition
    Result
    Continuous tracking of every identified person, deployed in the cloud or on the client’s premises.

  2. Municipal government · Road infrastructure

    Visual Truck Type Recognition

    Detect trucks driving on pavements their load can damage, without permanent human monitoring.

    Specification
    9 truck types classified by a custom-trained model.
    Technology
    Computer Vision · Image Analysis · Deep Learning
    Result
    No existing model could do the job. The system was integrated with the municipality’s CCTV and license plate recognition.

  3. Logistics · Warehousing

    WISE

    Warehouse Inventory System Expert

    Keep warehouse inventory without manual counts or data entry.

    Specification
    Up to 99% accuracy in product identification.
    Technology
    Computer Vision · Artificial Intelligence
    Result
    Real-time inventory from phone, drone or robot imagery, integrated with the client’s WMS.

  4. Retail · Point of sale

    Shelf Retail AI

    Audit the shelf in real time: assortment, stock-outs and planogram compliance.

    Specification
    Over 95% accuracy in audit results.
    Technology
    Computer Vision · Artificial Intelligence
    Built with
    LOGER · Vision Fractal
    Result
    Share of shelf, on-shelf availability (OSA/OOS) and planogram, measured automatically.

  5. Agriculture · Water management

    AgroTech — Rice Field Monitoring

    Decide irrigation for rice fields without relying on hours of manual aerial image analysis.

    Specification
    Aerial drone imagery in the visible and thermal spectrum.
    Technology
    Artificial Intelligence · Deep Learning · Computer Vision · Machine Vision
    Result
    Irrigation adjusted field zone by zone. The model was trained together with the client’s agronomists.

  6. Neuromarketing · User experience

    Deep Gaze© Eye Tracking

    Predict where a person looks at an ad or an interface, without consumer panels.

    Specification
    Accuracy in the order of 92% compared with a traditional eye-tracking study.
    Technology
    Computer Vision · Image Analysis · Transfer Learning
    Result
    Studies that took months, resolved in seconds. Trained on eye responses to more than 5,000 images.
